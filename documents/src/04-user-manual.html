1. What Zaka is

Zaka records what you spend, so that at the end of a month you can see where the money went. You type an amount, pick a category, and that is the whole of it.

Everything stays on your phone. There is no account to create, no password, and no server. Zaka makes no network connections at all: it cannot send your spending anywhere, because it has nothing to send it to and no permission to try.

What this version includes

Recording, listing and editing expenses, and monthly insights. Loyalty cards, the barcode scanner and the CSV export are not in this build — they are described in sections 7 and 8 so you know what is coming, and each says so plainly.

2. Getting started

2.1 Installing

  1. Install Zaka from Google Play, or from a build supplied to you directly.
  2. Open it. There is nothing to set up — no sign-in, no permissions to grant, no first-run questions.
  3. The Expenses tab opens, empty, with a short note telling you so.

If you are installing a new version over an old one, install over the top. Do not uninstall first; see section 9.

2.2 Finding your way around

Four tabs along the bottom, and one figure above them.

TabWhat it is for
ExpensesEverything you have recorded, newest first, grouped by day. This is where you edit and delete.
AddRecording something new.
InsightsWhat a month came to, and where it went.
CardsShop loyalty cards. Not in this build.

Just above the tabs, This month shows your running total for the current calendar month. It is there on every tab, deliberately: it is the one number worth seeing while you are doing something else.

3. Recording an expense

  1. Tap Add. The amount field is already selected and the number keypad is already open, so you can start typing immediately.
  2. Type the amount.
  3. Tap a category.
  4. Change the date if it was not today, and add a note if it is worth remembering.
  5. Tap Save. Zaka returns to the Expenses list with the new entry at the top.
Typing amounts: the comma

On a South African keypad the decimal key is a comma, and Zaka expects that. Both the comma and the full stop work as the decimal point:

You typeZaka recordsWhy
45,50R45.50One or two digits after the separator means cents.
45.50R45.50The same.
1,234R1,234.00Three digits after it, so that is a thousands separator.
1,234.56R1,234.56Both, read the way you meant them.
,50R0.50Fifty cents.

Amounts are always displayed as R1,234.56, whatever your phone's language and region are set to.

3.2 If Save will not work

Save stays greyed out until the amount is a number greater than zero. An empty field, letters, or a zero will keep it disabled. Nothing else on the form is required — you can save with no note and today's date.

4. The Expenses list

Everything you have recorded, newest first, under a heading for each day. The heading shows that day's total; today and yesterday are named rather than dated.

ToDo this
See the details of an expenseThey are all on the row: the note or category, the category name, and the amount.
Change an expenseTap the row. The same form opens with everything filled in. Tap Update.
Abandon a changeTap Cancel, or use the back gesture.
Delete an expenseSwipe the row from right to left. Or open it and tap Delete this expense.
Deleting cannot be undone

There is no undo and no confirmation in this version. A swipe removes the expense immediately and permanently. Swiping the other way does nothing, so an accidental brush is unlikely — but if you are not sure, open the row and read it before deleting.

Correcting an expense does not move it. If you fix an amount you typed wrong this morning, it stays where it was in this morning's list rather than jumping to the top.

5. Insights

The Insights tab answers two questions about one month.

  • What did the month come to — the total, at the top.
  • Where did it go — a donut or a set of bars, one per category, with each category's share as a percentage.

Underneath the total is Per day. For a month still running this divides by the days so far, not by the whole month, so the figure means "this is the rate I am going at" rather than being artificially low for most of the month.

5.1 Moving between months

The arrows beside the month name step back and forward. You cannot step past the current month, because there is nothing there. Stepping back far enough reaches whatever your earliest expense is; emptier months simply read zero.

5.2 Donut or bars

Use the switch under the total. Zaka remembers which you chose and opens that way next time.

Sixteen categories would be sixteen slivers and unreadable, so the chart keeps the five largest and adds the rest together as +N more. The total is never changed by that merging — the small categories are grouped, not dropped. The list underneath always names everything with its share and its amount, so if two colours look alike, read the list.

6. Reading the screen with TalkBack

Zaka is built to be used without sight.

  • Day headings are headings, so you can jump between days rather than swiping through every expense.
  • Rows read as one phrase. A day heading announces "Today, R150.00"; a chart row announces "Groceries, 37%, R3,170.00" — not as a series of disconnected fragments.
  • Delete does not need the swipe. Each expense carries an action called "Delete this expense" in TalkBack's actions menu, which does exactly what the swipe does.
  • Every icon-only control is labelled — the month arrows announce as "Previous month" and "Next month".
  • Colour is never the only signal. Every category colour is paired with a symbol and its name in text.

If you use a large system font and something is cut off or cramped, that is a fault worth reporting — it is not yet verified at the largest sizes.

7. Loyalty cards not in this build

A coming version will keep your shop loyalty cards so the plastic can stay at home. You will be able to add a card by typing its number or scanning the barcode with the camera, and tap a card to show it full screen as a barcode for the till to read — with the screen brightness turned up so the scanner can see it, and a QR option for the same number.

Loyalty cards only. Never put a bank or credit card number into Zaka. What Zaka stores is not encrypted, which is fine for a shop card's number and is not fine for anything that can take money.

8. Exporting your expenses not in this build

A coming version will export everything as a CSV file you can share to email, Files, or a spreadsheet. The amounts will be plain numbers so a spreadsheet adds them up rather than treating them as text.

9. Looking after your data

Zaka's database is the only copy of your expenses. There is no server holding a second one.

SituationWhat happens
You restart the phoneNothing. Your data is on the device.
You install a new version over the old oneNothing. Your data is kept.
You uninstall ZakaYour expenses are destroyed. Android removes the app's data with the app.
You back the phone upZaka's database is included, if you have Android's backup turned on.
You move to a new phoneThe database comes across with the backup. Check that your expenses are there before wiping the old phone.
Before anything risky

Before uninstalling, resetting, or moving to a new phone, make sure Android's backup is on and has run. Once the export in section 8 exists, export a CSV and mail it to yourself as well.

10. If something looks wrong

SymptomLikely reasonWhat to do
An amount is a hundred times too bigA separator was read as grouping rather than as a decimal point — for instance 4550 typed without one.Tap the row and correct it. See section 3 for how separators are read.
An expense is under the wrong dayThe date was left as today when the spending happened earlier.Tap the row and change the date.
"This month" looks too lowIt counts the current calendar month only, not the last thirty days.Use Insights and step back a month to see the rest.
Two identical expensesSaved twice.Delete one. This was a defect in an early build and is fixed.
Two chart colours look alikeSixteen categories, a limited palette.Read the list under the chart; it names every slice.
Everything is goneThe app was uninstalled and reinstalled.Restore from a device backup if you have one. There is nothing else to recover from, and that is the trade for having no server.

11. Your privacy, in full

  • Zaka collects nothing and sends nothing.
  • It has no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising.
  • It has no network permission, so it could not phone home if it wanted to.
  • Your expenses are stored in Zaka's own private area on your phone, and included in your device backup if you have one.
  • Nobody — including whoever wrote it — can see what you spend.
  • The trade for that is honest: if the data is lost from your phone and your backup, it is gone.
